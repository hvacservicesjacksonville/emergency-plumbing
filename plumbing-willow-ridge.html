How We Execute Plumbing Work in Willow Ridge

Every Willow Ridge job starts with the same question: what is actually failing? We do not guess. A pressure gauge on the hose bib tells us if the problem is supply-side. A sewer camera tells us if the drain issue is root

01

Hard water scaling water heaters and fixtures

Willow Ridge water leaves mineral scale in tanks, aerators and tankless heat exchangers. That shortens water heater life and causes low water pressure at fixtures. It also makes faucet cartridges stick and toilets run. How we help: We test hardness at the tap, recommend a properly sized water softener, and descale tankless units with a pump kit during service. On water heater calls we flush sediment before we replace parts, so the fix lasts.

02

Frozen and split supply lines in cold snaps

When temperatures drop below zero, uninsulated lines in garage walls and crawlspaces freeze. Copper splits, PEX can crack at fittings, and you get water where you do not want it. These calls come in at 2 a.m. and on holidays. How we help: We isolate the line, thaw it safely, cut out the split section and repair with new copper or PEX. We also insulate the run and add heat tape where it makes sense, so the same line does not freeze again next winter.

03

Sump pump failure during spring thaw

Willow Ridge basements sit on soil that holds snowmelt and heavy rain. When a sump pump float sticks or the check valve fails, water backs into the pit and can reach the floor. This is a fast-moving problem. How we help: We test the float and check valve, clear the discharge line and verify the pump moves water. If the pump is done, we install a new one sized to the pit and route the discharge away from the foundation.
