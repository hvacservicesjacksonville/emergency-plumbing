Older cast iron sewer lines with root intrusion

Dunham Park has mature trees and decades-old clay or cast iron laterals. Roots find the joints and grow in, causing repeat backups even after snaking. A snake clears the line today, but the root is back in months. How we help: We camera the line first to see the actual condition, then hydro jet the roots out and cut them at the source. If the line is cracked or shifted, we quote a spot repair or replacement instead of selling you another snake job that will not hold.

02

Frozen pipes in exterior walls and crawlspaces

Dunham Park winters drop well below freezing, and older homes here have plumbing in exterior walls and unheated crawlspaces. A hard cold snap can freeze a supply line overnight, and the thaw is when it bursts. How we help: We thaw lines with controlled heat, not open flame, and then insulate or reroute the vulnerable section. If the pipe has already split, we cut out the damaged section and replace it with PEX or copper. We also check the shut-off valve so you can isolate the line next time.

03

Hard water scaling tankless and tank water heaters

Dunham Park water carries enough mineral content that tankless heat exchangers scale up within a couple of years. Tank heaters get sediment buildup that shortens their life and raises energy bills. How we help: We descale tankless units with a pump kit and flush tank heaters annually. For homes without a softener, we test the water and quote a softener install. That one change usually doubles the service life of the water heater and the fixtures.

04

Sewer backups during spring thaw and heavy rain

Spring thaw and summer storms push a lot of water into Dunham Park's older combined storm and sanitary lines. When the main surcharges, basements with floor drains or weak check valves get backup water. How we help: We install or repair sump pumps and check valves, and we camera the lateral to confirm it is not already compromised.
