What's Included in Our Pipe Replacement Service

Every pipe replacement job includes the essentials: removal of old pipe, installation of new copper or PEX, new shut-off valves, and a full pressure test. We also handle permit paperwork when required. Add-ons like drywall repair or fixture upgrades are quoted separately so you only pay for what you need.

01

Full Pipe Removal and Disposal

We cut out every foot of failing pipe and haul it away. No piles of old galvanized steel in your yard, no debris left in your crawl space. You get a clean slate and a clean property when we're done.

02

New Copper or PEX Installation

We install copper pipe or PEX tubing based on your home's layout and your preference. Copper lasts decades and handles high heat; PEX is flexible, freeze-resistant, and costs less. We'll explain the trade-offs and let you choose.

03

New Shut-Off Valves and Fittings

Every replacement includes new shut-off valves at the main and at each fixture group. We use quality pipe fittings, Teflon tape, and plumber's putty where needed. Old, frozen valves are a common failure point, so we replace them while we're in there.

04

Pressure Testing and Leak Check

After installation, we pressurize the system and check every joint with a pressure gauge. We run water at every fixture and inspect for drips. You see the results before we pack up. If anything fails, we fix it on the spot.

05

Permit Handling and Inspection

When Sioux Falls requires a permit for pipe replacement, we pull it and schedule the inspection. Our work meets Uniform Plumbing Code and International Plumbing Code standards, so it passes without a hitch. You get documentation for your records.

Tools & methods

Tools and Methods We Use for Pipe Replacement

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

Pipe Cutter

Cuts copper, PEX, and PVC cleanly without crushing the pipe. Clean cuts mean better joints and fewer leaks down the road.

02

Soldering Torch

Used for copper pipe joints. We apply flux and solder to create a permanent, leak-free seal that holds up under pressure and temperature swings.

03

PEX Crimping Tool

Secures PEX tubing to fittings with a uniform crimp. A proper crimp is the difference between a 50-year connection and a callback.

04

Pressure Gauge

Measures water pressure at multiple points in the system. We use it before and after replacement to confirm the new pipe is delivering full flow.

05

Leak Detection Microphone

Listens for the sound of water escaping behind walls or under slabs. It lets us pinpoint hidden leaks without tearing up your home.

06

Sewer Camera

Inspects drain and sewer lines from the inside. We use it to check for root intrusion, collapsed sections, or debris before and after replacement.

How much does pipe replacement cost in Sioux Falls SD?

Most single-line replacements run $450 to $1,800, while a whole-home repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on house size and pipe material. Main water line replacement Sioux Falls projects often land between $3,000 and $8,000. Factors include accessibility, whether we're cutting drywall or concrete, and the length of the run. We quote upfront before any work begins.

When should I replace my pipes in Sioux Falls South Dakota?

Replace pipes when you see rusty water, recurring leaks, or pressure that keeps dropping, or when your home still has galvanized steel or polybutylene. Galvanized pipe typically fails between 40 and 60 years. Polybutylene, common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid-1990s, gets brittle and fails at fittings. If you're seeing any of these signs, schedule an inspection before winter.

How long does pipe replacement take in Sioux Falls?

A single line takes two to four hours. A whole-home repipe usually runs one to three days for an average Sioux Falls house. Larger homes or jobs requiring significant drywall or concrete work can stretch to four or five days. We give you a realistic timeline during the quote and keep you updated if anything changes once walls are open.

Do you replace galvanized pipes in Sioux Falls SD?

Yes, galvanized pipe replacement is one of our most common jobs. We cut out the old steel, install copper or PEX in its place, and upgrade shut-off valves while we're in there. Most galvanized systems in Sioux Falls homes built before 1960 are past their service life. We can usually complete the swap in one to two days.
