What Our Tankless Installation Includes

Every tankless job we quote covers the unit, the permit, the labor, and the startup. Here is what is in the price, and what we handle as an add-on when your home needs it.

01

Unit and Mounting

We supply and mount a Rheem, Navien, Rinnai, or A.O. Smith condensing unit on a rated bracket. Includes the condensate drain line, neutralizer, and a dedicated 120V circuit for the control board.

06

Startup and Walkthrough

Burner fired, outlet temperature set to 120 degrees, every hot fixture tested at once. We hand you the manual, warranty card, and a descaling schedule, and we haul away the old tank at no extra charge.

Tools & methods

Tools and Methods We Use

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

RIDGID Pipe Wrench and Cutter

Breaking and re-making gas and water connections on black iron and copper. A clean cut means a square seat and no leaks at the joint.

02

PEX Crimping Tool

Making ASTM-rated crimp connections on PEX tubing runs. Faster than soldering and just as reliable when the ring is set to spec.

03

Soldering Torch and Flux

Sweating copper pipe joints on the hot and cold tie-ins. We use lead-free solder and clean flux, then pressure-test every joint at 100 PSI.

04

Pressure Gauge

Confirming incoming water pressure and holding the system at test pressure before we close up. A gauge catches a pinhole leak before it becomes a wall stain.

05

Leak Detection Microphone

Listening for hidden leaks in walls and under slabs before we tie a new heater into a failing line. It finds the problem the eye cannot.

06

Milwaukee Cordless Tools

Drilling vent penetrations, driving brackets, and cutting pipe in tight mechanical closets where a cord will not reach.

07

Combustion Analyzer

Verifying CO and O2 levels at startup on every condensing unit. It confirms the burner is running clean before we sign off.

Pricing process

Tankless Water Heater Installation Cost

Phase 01

Gas line size and run

If your home has a 1/2-inch stub, we upsize to 3/4-inch black iron or CSST. A short run in an open basement adds $250 to $500. A long run through finished space or from an undersized meter adds more.

Phase 02

Vent configuration

Sidewall venting is simpler and cheaper than a roof penetration. If your mechanical room is interior, we may need a longer concentric vent run, which adds $300 to $700 in materials and labor.

Phase 03

Unit capacity and brand

A 199,000 BTU condensing unit from Navien or Rinnai costs more than a mid-range Rheem, but it handles colder inlet water better. We match the unit to your fixture count so you are not paying for capacity you will never use.

How much does tankless water heater installation cost in Sioux Falls SD?

Most installations run $2,800 to $4,600 in 2026, including the unit, permit, and labor. A straight swap with an existing 3/4-inch gas line sits near the low end. If we have to upsize the gas supply, add a vent run through a finished ceiling, or install a recirculation loop, expect the upper range. Electric tankless conversions often land $400 to $900 lower because there is no venting.

Is a tankless water heater worth it in Sioux Falls South Dakota?

Yes, if you plan to stay in the home more than five years. Our hard water shortens tank life to 8 to 12 years, so a tankless unit at 18 to 22 years avoids one full replacement. You also cut water-heating gas use by 22 to 30 percent. The break-even point for a family of four is usually year five or six.

How long does tankless water heater installation take in Sioux Falls?

A standard conversion takes four to six hours on install day. Add a half-day if we need to run a new gas line from the meter or cut a new vent penetration through the roof or sidewall. The permit and inspection add two to four business days to the schedule, but the work itself is a one-day job for most Sioux Falls homes.

Do I need a permit for tankless water heater installation in Sioux Falls SD?

Yes. The City of Sioux Falls requires a mechanical permit for any water heater replacement, tank or tankless, and the work must meet Uniform Plumbing Code and International Plumbing Code. We pull the permit for you and meet the inspector on site. Unpermitted installs can void your homeowner insurance and cause problems when you sell.
