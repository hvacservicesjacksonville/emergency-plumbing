Hard water scaling up water heaters and fixtures

Coventry's water leaves mineral deposits inside tanks, heat exchangers, and aerators. That shortens water heater life and slows flow at faucets and showerheads. How we help: We test water hardness on site and recommend a correctly sized water softener. For tankless units, we descale the heat exchanger and set a flushing schedule so you are not replacing the unit early.

02

Frozen pipes in garages and crawl spaces

Coventry winters drop well below freezing, and pipes in unheated spaces are the first to freeze. A frozen line can split and flood when it thaws. How we help: We thaw frozen lines with controlled heat, repair any split pipe with copper or PEX, and insulate or heat-tape the run so it does not happen again next winter.

Spring thaw overwhelms sump pumps

When snow melts fast around Coventry, sump pumps run nonstop. Older pumps burn out or float switches stick, and basements take on water. How we help: We test the float, check valve, and discharge line, replace failed pumps, and confirm the discharge runs away from the foundation so water does not cycle back in.
