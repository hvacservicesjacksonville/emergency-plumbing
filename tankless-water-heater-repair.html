Service overview

What Tankless Water Heater Repair Sioux Falls SD Involves

Tankless water heater repair Sioux Falls SD is the diagnostic and parts-replacement work that restores a tankless unit to full output when it stops heating, throws an error code, or delivers fluctuating temperatures. It covers descaling the heat exchanger, replacing flow sensors, cleaning or swapping igniters, testing inlet and outlet thermistors, and repairing gas valve or heating element faults. Unlike a tank swap, tankless work is component-level: we test each part with a multimeter and pressure gauge, isolate the failure, and replace only what has actually failed.

In Sioux Falls, most tankless failures trace back to hard water scale and cold incoming supply. Our water runs mineral-heavy, and a heat exchanger that scales up loses efficiency fast, then overheats and shuts down. Winter makes it worse: incoming water near 40 degrees forces the unit to fire harder, and a partially scaled exchanger cannot keep up. Tools & methods

Tools and Methods We Use

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

Descaling Pump

Circulates a vinegar or commercial descaling solution through the heat exchanger to dissolve mineral scale. This is the core tool for restoring output on hard-water units.

02

Digital Multimeter

Tests voltage and resistance at the control board, flow sensor, and thermistors. It tells us which component has actually failed instead of replacing parts on suspicion.

03

Gas Pressure Gauge

Measures inlet and manifold gas pressure on gas-fired units. Low or high pressure causes ignition faults and incomplete combustion that mimic a failed part.

04

Pipe Wrench and Pipe Cutter

Used to break loose supply connections and cut out damaged pipe when we replace a fitting or a section of copper pipe feeding the unit.

05

PEX Crimping Tool

Crimps PEX tubing connections when we repair or replace supply lines. PEX is common in newer Sioux Falls homes and holds up well against hard water.

06

Combustion Analyzer

Checks flue gas on gas tankless units after a repair. It confirms the burner is running clean and the venting is doing its job before we sign off.

Problems we solve

Tankless Problems We Fix Every Week

These are the calls we get most in Sioux Falls. If any of them sound familiar, you are not alone, and none of them are a reason to replace the unit.

ISSUE

No hot water at all from the tap

We test the ignition sequence, confirm gas pressure at the valve, and check for a lockout code. Most no-heat calls are fixed with an igniter or flow sensor replacement, and we carry both on the truck.

SYMPTOM

Water goes hot then cold mid-shower

We measure flow rate and inlet temperature, then check the mixing valve and the flow sensor. A scaled exchanger is often the culprit, and a descale plus a sensor check usually settles it.

SIGN

Error code flashing on the panel

We read the manufacturer fault list, test the matching component, and confirm the repair. If you need tankless water heater error code repair Sioux Falls, that is a standard call for us.

ALERT

Unit runs but water never gets hot enough

We descale the exchanger, test both thermistors, and verify the temperature setpoint and gas input. Most units recover full output after a flush and a sensor replacement.

How much does tankless water heater repair cost in Sioux Falls SD?

Most tankless repairs in Sioux Falls run $180 to $650, with a diagnostic fee starting at $89 that we credit toward the repair. A flow sensor or igniter swap sits at the low end. A control board, gas valve, or heat exchanger replacement pushes toward the top. Descaling alone is usually $150 to $250 and often restores full output without any parts.

Why is my tankless water heater not working in Sioux Falls South Dakota?

The three most common causes here are scale buildup in the heat exchanger, a failed flow sensor, and a dirty or worn igniter. Hard Sioux Falls water scales the exchanger faster than in softer-water cities, and cold winter inlet temperatures make a scaled unit overheat and shut down. If your tankless water heater is not heating, start by checking the error code on the panel.

Can you repair a tankless water heater in Sioux Falls?

Yes. We repair gas and electric tankless units from Rheem, Navien, Rinnai, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, and American Standard across Sioux Falls and Minnehaha County. We carry OEM parts on the truck, so most repairs finish the same day. If the unit is under manufacturer warranty, we can handle the parts claim as well.

How often should tankless water heaters be serviced in Sioux Falls SD?

Once a year, and every six to nine months if you have not installed a water softener. Sioux Falls water is hard enough that scale builds quickly, and an annual descale plus a flow and temperature check keeps the unit at full output. Skipping service is the fastest way to a $600 heat exchanger replacement instead of a $200 flush.
