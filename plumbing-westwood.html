How We Execute Plumbing Work in Westwood

Plumbing work in Westwood follows the same code requirements as the rest of Sioux Falls, but the execution details are local. We pull permits through Minnehaha County when the job requires it, and we work to the Uniform

01

Hard water scaling inside water heaters and fixtures

Westwood water carries enough minerals to shorten water heater life and clog aerators. Scale builds on heating elements and inside tankless heat exchangers, which means more repairs and shorter equipment lifespan. How we help: We test water hardness on call-outs and recommend a softener when the numbers justify it. For tankless units, we descale the heat exchanger and set a maintenance schedule so the unit lasts.

02

Frozen pipes in garages and crawl spaces

Westwood homes often have supply lines running through unheated garages or crawl spaces. A sustained cold snap can freeze those lines and split copper or PEX, causing water damage when they thaw. How we help: We insulate exposed lines, reroute where possible, and install shut-off valves so a freeze does not flood the house. We also show homeowners where the main shut-off is before we leave.

03

Sump pump failure during spring melt

Westwood basements rely on sump pumps to handle snowmelt and heavy spring rain. A failed float switch or clogged discharge line can leave standing water in a finished basement within hours. How we help: We test the pump, check the check valve, and clear the discharge line. If the pump is older, we recommend a backup system so the basement stays dry during the next storm.
