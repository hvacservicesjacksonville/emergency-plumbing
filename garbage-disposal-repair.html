What's included

What Our Garbage Disposal Repair Includes

Every repair call includes a full diagnostic, the fix itself, and a test run before we leave. Here's what's covered in the standard price and what counts as an add-on.

01

Full Diagnostic Test

We test the outlet, reset button, motor windings, and flywheel rotation. You get a clear explanation of what failed and why. This takes about 15 minutes and is included in every repair.

02

Jam Removal and Reset

If the disposal is jammed, we free the flywheel with a hex wrench, remove the obstruction, and reset the unit. We run a 60-second test cycle to confirm it spins clean. This is the most common repair we do.

03

Capacitor or Motor Repair

A humming disposal usually needs a new capacitor or a motor brush replacement. We stock both for common brands and install them on-site. Most motor repairs take 45 to 60 minutes.

04

Leak Repair and Reseal

We replace failed flange gaskets, discharge tube seals, and plumber's putty rings. Every seal is pressure-tested with a full sink of water before we call the job done. Leak repairs carry the same 1-year warranty.

05

Drain Line Check

We inspect the P-trap and discharge tube for blockages that could back up into the disposal. If we find a clog, we clear it with a drain snake or auger. This prevents repeat failures and protects your new parts.

06

Cleanup and Haul-Away

We lay a drop cloth, wipe down the cabinet floor, and take old parts with us. You don't have to deal with the mess. The work area is cleaner when we leave than when we arrived.

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

RIDGID Drain Snake

Clears clogs in the P-trap and branch line when a disposal backs up. We use the 1/4-inch and 3/8-inch cables depending on the pipe size.

Milwaukee Multimeter

Tests outlet voltage, motor windings, and capacitor function. This tells us whether the motor is salvageable or needs replacement.

Problems we solve

Common Disposal Problems We Fix in Sioux Falls

Most disposal failures fall into five categories. Here's what we see most often and how we fix it.

ISSUE

Disposal hums but won't spin

We cut power, insert a 1/4-inch hex wrench into the bottom socket, and work the flywheel free in both directions. Then we remove the obstruction with tongs and run a test cycle. Most jams clear in 20 minutes.

SYMPTOM

Water leaks from the bottom of the disposal

We pull the disposal, inspect the flange gasket and discharge tube, and replace the failed seal with plumber's putty and a new gasket. We pressure-test with a full sink of water before we leave.

SIGN

Disposal is completely dead, no sound

We test the outlet with a multimeter, check the breaker, and inspect the motor windings. If the motor is gone, we quote a replacement on the spot and install it the same day.

ALERT

Disposal drains slowly or backs up

We clear the P-trap and run a drain snake or auger through the branch line. If the clog is further down, we use a sewer camera to find the source. Most clogs clear in under an hour.

Garbage Disposal Repair Pricing

Phase 01

Type of failure

A jam removal is the cheapest repair at $180-$220. A capacitor or motor repair runs $220-$300. A flange leak repair with resealing sits around $240-$350. The more we have to disassemble, the higher the labor.

Phase 02

Disposal brand and model

Parts for Moen, Delta, and InSinkErator are easy to source and affordable. Older or off-brand units may need special-order parts, which adds $30-$60 and can delay the repair by a day.

Phase 03

Access under the sink

A clean, open cabinet makes the job faster. If the disposal is buried behind a garbage can, cleaning supplies, or a tight pipe configuration, expect an extra 15-20 minutes of labor.

Why is my garbage disposal not working in Sioux Falls South Dakota?

The three most common causes are a jammed flywheel, a tripped internal breaker, or a burned-out motor. Hard water scale in Sioux Falls dulls the shredder ring and makes jams more likely. If the unit hums without spinning, it's jammed. If it's silent, check the reset button and the breaker. If both are fine, the motor is likely dead and needs a replacement.

Can you repair a jammed garbage disposal in Sioux Falls?
