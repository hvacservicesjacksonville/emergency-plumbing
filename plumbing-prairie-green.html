Hard water scaling fixtures and water heaters

Prairie Green water runs hard, so faucet cartridges, water heater elements and tankless heat exchangers scale up faster than in softer-water areas. How we help: We test hardness on site, descale tankless units during repair, and recommend a properly sized water softener. Freeze-thaw stress on exterior and garage lines

South Dakota winters push frost deep, and lines running through garages or along exterior walls in Prairie Green are the first to freeze. A frozen line can split and flood when it thaws. How we help: We thaw lines safely without a torch on PEX, then repair or reroute the vulnerable section.
