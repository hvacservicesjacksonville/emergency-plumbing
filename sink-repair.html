What our sink repair service includes

Every sink repair call includes a full inspection, a flat price before work starts, and a test of the finished repair. Here is what is covered. If we find something outside this scope, we tell you before we touch it and quote the extra work separately.

01

Full under-sink inspection

We check the trap, supply lines, shut-off valves, disposal, and drain assembly. We run water, watch for drips, and use a leak detection microphone if the source is hidden. You see what we find before we quote the repair.

02

Clog removal

We clear sink clogs with a drain snake, auger, or hydro jetter depending on the blockage. Grease, soap scum, and mineral buildup come out fast. If the clog is in the main line, we scope it with a sewer camera to find the real cause.

05

Post-repair testing and cleanup

We fill both bowls, run the disposal, and check every joint with a dry rag. The drain runs for 5 to 10 minutes to confirm no weep. We wipe down the cabinet, haul away old parts, and walk you through what we fixed.

Tools & methods

Tools and methods we use

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

Drain snake and auger

Clears grease, soap scum, and mineral clogs from sink branch lines. We use a hand auger for shallow clogs and a powered drum snake for deeper blockages.

02

Hydro jetter

Blasts through heavy grease and scale with high-pressure water. We use it when snaking alone will not clear the line or when the clog keeps returning.

03

Sewer camera

Feeds through the branch line and main sewer to find root intrusion, cracks, or bellies. You get a video recording and a written report of what we found.

04

Leak detection microphone

Picks up the sound of water escaping behind walls or under cabinets. We use it when the leak source is hidden and we want to avoid tearing out the whole cabinet.

05

Pipe wrench and pipe cutter

Loosens frozen fittings and cuts out corroded copper or ABS pipe. We carry both in every van so we can replace a damaged section on the spot.

06

Soldering torch and PEX crimping tool

Sweats new copper joints or crimps PEX tubing connections. We match the repair method to the pipe material already in your home.

07

Pressure gauge

Tests the repaired line under pressure to confirm no joint weeps. We hold pressure for several minutes before we call the job done.

Problems we solve

Sink problems we solve in Sioux Falls

Most sink calls fall into a handful of categories. Knowing which one you have helps us bring the right parts and finish in one visit. Here are the problems we see most often in Sioux Falls homes.

ISSUE

Slow drain that pools water in the bowl

We run a drain snake or auger through the branch line to clear grease, soap scum, and mineral buildup. For recurring clogs, we scope the line with a sewer camera to check for root intrusion or a bellied pipe. Most clogs clear in under an hour.

SYMPTOM

Leaking supply line or shut-off valve

We replace worn supply lines with braided stainless or PEX tubing and swap out failed shut-off valves. Every connection gets Teflon tape and a pressure test. Most repairs take 1 to 2 hours, and we check the other sink while we are there.

SIGN

Cracked P-trap or drain assembly

We cut out the cracked trap and install a new PVC or ABS P-trap with fresh plumber's putty seals. We check the tailpiece and drain flange for corrosion while the trap is off. The repair takes about an hour and includes a full flow test.

ALERT

Garbage disposal humming but not spinning

We cut power, clear the jam with an auger, and reset the unit. If the motor is burned out, we install a new disposal in about 90 minutes. We test the dishwasher drain connection before we leave.

Type of failure

Hard water is the usual culprit. It eats rubber washers, O-rings, and plumber's putty seals until they fail.
