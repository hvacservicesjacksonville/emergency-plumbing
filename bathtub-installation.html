What's included

What Our Bathtub Installation Includes

Every tub job we quote covers the work needed to remove the old unit and leave you with a working, code-correct tub. Here is what is included in a standard install, and what we price separately if your bathroom needs it.

01

Old Tub Removal

We disconnect the drain and overflow, cut the caulk line, and remove the old tub. Cast iron gets cut into sections so it comes out without damaging tile or door frames. The old unit and debris are hauled away the same day.

02

New Tub Set and Level

The new tub goes onto a mortar or foam bed and is checked with a level in both directions. We dry-fit first to confirm the drain lines up, then set it permanent. That is what stops the flex and squeak you get from a tub set on bare subfloor.

03

Drain and Overflow Connection

We install a new waste and overflow assembly with fresh gaskets, set the trap arm at the correct slope, and pressure-test the joint. If the existing trap arm is flat or damaged, we replace it with PVC pipe and fittings before the tub goes in.

04

Supply Line Hookup

Hot and cold supply lines get connected with new washers and Teflon tape on the threads. If your shut-off valves are old gate valves that will not seal, we swap them for quarter-turn ball valves so you can isolate the tub later without shutting down the house.

05

Seal, Test, and Cleanup

We fill the tub, watch the drain and overflow for leaks, and run hot water through both supplies. The surround gets caulked with plumber's putty and silicone, drywall dust gets vacuumed, and we walk the job with you before we leave.

06

Haul-Away and Disposal

The old tub, old drain parts, and any packaging leave with us. You do not need a dumpster, a helper, or a trip to the landfill. That is included in every bathtub replacement Sioux Falls SD job we quote.

Tools & methods

Tools and Methods We Use on Tub Installs

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

Pipe wrench and pipe cutter

Used to break loose old drain fittings and cut copper or PVC pipe cleanly when we replace the trap arm or supply lines behind the tub.

02

Soldering torch and PEX crimping tool

For copper pipe we sweat joints with a soldering torch and flux. For PEX tubing we use a crimping tool to set rings. Both give a permanent, leak-free connection.

03

Drain snake and hydro jetter

We clear slow or blocked tub drains before the new unit goes in. A snake handles most clogs; a hydro jetter scours grease and scale from the branch line.

04

Leak detection microphone and pressure gauge

If we suspect a hidden supply leak behind the tub wall, we listen with a microphone and confirm with a pressure gauge before the new tub is set.

05

Sewer camera

A camera inspection shows whether the drain line under the tub is clear and sound. It is a quick check that prevents a nasty surprise after the install.

06

RIDGID and Milwaukee cordless tools

Our techs use RIDGID and Milwaukee saws, drivers, and inspection cameras for cutting cast iron, setting blocking, and confirming the drain alignment.

Problems we solve

Bathtub Problems We Solve for Sioux Falls Homeowners

Most tub replacements start with one of these failures. We fix the cause, not just the symptom, so your new tub does not repeat the problem.

ISSUE

Rusted drain and leaking overflow

We cut out the old waste and overflow, inspect the subfloor and joists, and install a new assembly with fresh gaskets. We pressure-test the joint before the surround goes back on, so the leak is gone for good.

SYMPTOM

Cracked acrylic or flexing tub floor

We remove the old unit, repair any soft subfloor, and set the new tub on a mortar or foam bed. That eliminates the flex and gives you a solid floor that will not crack at the drain.

SIGN

Slow drain or standing water

We clear the line with a drain snake or hydro jetter, check the trap arm slope with a level, and confirm the vent is clear. Your new tub drains in under two minutes instead of pooling at your ankles.

ALERT

Old gate valves that will not shut off

We replace old gate valves with quarter-turn ball valves during the install. You get a working shut-off at the tub, so future service does not require draining the whole system.

Pricing process

Bathtub Installation Cost in Sioux Falls

$1,200 - $8,000 depending on tub type, subfloor condition, and finish work

Phase 01

Tub type and material

A standard acrylic alcove tub is the least expensive option. Cast iron, freestanding, and walk-in tubs cost more because of the unit price, the weight, and the extra labor to set and connect them correctly.

Phase 02

Subfloor condition

If the old drain has been leaking, the subfloor and sometimes the joists need repair. We patch and level before the new tub goes in. That is a real cost, but skipping it means the new tub will flex and the drain seal will fail.

Phase 03

Drain and supply location

If the new tub's drain does not line up with the existing trap arm, we relocate it. Moving supply lines for a freestanding filler adds labor and materials. We confirm the layout before we quote so the price is accurate.
