Part Replacement

Tank Flush

Sioux Falls hard water leaves scale behind. We flush the tank to remove sediment that causes popping and shortens tank life. This step is included on most repairs and takes about 20 minutes. It helps your unit run quieter and last longer.

The anode rod sacrifices itself to protect the tank from rust. We pull and inspect it on every call. If it is more than 50 percent consumed, we replace it. That simple step can add years to your water heater.

06

Hot-Water Verification

We run the unit through a full heat cycle and measure the temperature at the nearest fixture. It should read between 120 and 140 degrees. You confirm the hot water is back before we leave. That is the final step on every job.

Problems we solve

Water Heater Problems We Fix in Sioux Falls

Most water heater failures fall into a few categories. Here is what we see most in Sioux Falls and how we fix it fast.

ISSUE

No hot water at all

We test the thermocouple, pilot, and reset button first. Most no-hot-water calls are a failed thermocouple or a gas valve issue. We carry both parts and fix it in under two hours. You get hot water back the same day.

SYMPTOM

Rusty or metallic-smelling hot water

We pull the anode rod and inspect the tank interior. If the tank is still sound, we replace the rod and flush the sediment. That stops the rust and adds years to the tank. If the tank is compromised, we tell you straight.

SIGN

Popping or rumbling sounds

We flush the tank with a full-volume drain and clear the sediment. If the buildup is heavy, we use a descaling solution. After the flush, the popping stops and the unit runs quieter. We also check the anode rod while we are in there.

ALERT

Water pooling around the tank

We dry the area and trace the leak. If it is a fitting or Teflon tape joint, we tighten or re-tape it. If the tank itself is leaking, we show you the crack and quote a same-day replacement. We also check the shut-off valve.

Pricing process

Water Heater Repair Cost in Sioux Falls

$180 - $450 for most repairs; $1,200 - $2,500 for replacement

Phase 01

Type of failure

A thermocouple or element is a quick, low-cost fix. A gas valve, blower motor, or control board costs more because the part is expensive and the labor takes longer. We diagnose the failure and quote the exact price before we start.

Phase 02

Gas vs. electric

Gas water heaters have more complex controls, including a gas valve and thermocouple. Electric units use elements and thermostats. Gas repairs usually cost slightly more because of the additional safety testing required.

Phase 03

Tank age and condition

If the tank is under 10 years old and the failure is a part, repair is straightforward. If the tank is corroded or leaking, replacement is the only option. We show you the corrosion and give you both numbers.

Why is my water heater not working in Sioux Falls South Dakota?

Should I repair or replace my water heater in Sioux Falls?
