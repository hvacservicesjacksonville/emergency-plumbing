You call our 24/7 line and speak to a live dispatcher. We ask what is happening, get your address, and send the nearest truck. You get a text with the technician name and estimated arrival time. Most calls dispatch within 10 minutes.

02

On-Site Diagnosis

The plumber arrives, shuts off the water if needed, and inspects the failure point. We use a pressure gauge, leak detection microphone, or sewer camera to pinpoint the problem. You get a clear explanation and a flat-rate price before any work begins.

Repair or Replace

We fix the issue using code-correct methods and materials. That might mean soldering copper pipe, crimping PEX tubing, or replacing a failed wax ring. If a shut-off valve is corroded, we swap it. The goal is a permanent fix, not a patch.

Tools and Methods We Use

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

Pipe Wrench and Auger

Used to loosen fittings and clear tough clogs in drains. We carry RIDGID and Milwaukee brands for reliability.

02

Hydro Jetter

High-pressure water clears grease, roots, and debris from sewer lines. It is safe for pipes and leaves them clean.

03

Sewer Camera

We inspect the inside of sewer lines to find cracks, roots, and blockages. You see the footage and understand the problem.

04

Leak Detection Microphone

Amplifies the sound of leaking water behind walls or under slabs, helping us pinpoint hidden leaks without demolition.

05

PEX Crimping Tool

Used to make secure connections on PEX tubing. It creates a reliable, code-approved seal.

06

Soldering Torch

For copper pipe repairs, we use a torch to solder joints. This creates a strong, permanent connection.
