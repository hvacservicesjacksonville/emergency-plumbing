Tools & methods

Tools and Methods We Use for Pipe Repair

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

Sewer Camera

We push a high-resolution camera through your sewer line to see cracks, roots, and blockages. This lets us pinpoint the problem without digging.

02

Leak Detection Microphone

This sensitive device picks up the sound of water escaping pipes behind walls or under slabs, helping us locate hidden leaks fast.

03

Pipe Wrench and Auger

We use heavy-duty pipe wrenches to loosen fittings and augers to clear stubborn clogs in drain lines.

04

Hydro Jetter

High-pressure water scours the inside of pipes, removing grease, scale, and roots. It's safe for most pipe materials when used correctly.

05

Soldering Torch and PEX Crimping Tool

For copper repairs, we solder joints with a torch. For PEX, we use a crimping tool to secure fittings. Both methods create reliable, code-compliant connections.

06

Pressure Gauge

We attach a pressure gauge to test your system after repairs, ensuring there are no hidden leaks and pressure is within normal range.
