01

Hard water scaling inside pipes and water heaters

Scale builds on elements, fixtures, and tank interiors, shortening water heater life and reducing flow at every tap. It also makes tankless units cycle and shut off mid-use if they are not descaled regularly. How we help: We test water hardness at the tap, recommend a correctly sized softener, and descale tankless heat exchangers during service. For water heaters, we flush sediment and replace anode rods to slow corrosion. On repipes, we size PEX tubing to handle the flow loss scale causes over time.

02

Frozen and split pipes during cold snaps

Exposed lines in crawlspaces, garages, and along exterior walls freeze when temperatures drop below zero. A split copper or PEX line releases water fast once it thaws, and the damage is often worse than the repair. How we help: We thaw lines with controlled heat, locate the split with a pressure gauge and visual inspection, and cut out the damaged section. Repairs use matched material and new fittings, then we pressure-test before restoring water. We also insulate or reroute lines that freeze every year.

03

Root intrusion in older sewer laterals

Trees near established streets push roots through pipe joints, causing slow drains, backups, and eventually full blockages. Roots find the same joints year after year, so the problem returns if the pipe is not fixed properly. How we help: We run a sewer camera to see the intrusion, then cut roots with an auger or hydro jetter. If joints are damaged, we repair or replace that section with new PVC pipe and proper slope. We show you the camera footage so you can see exactly what we found.
