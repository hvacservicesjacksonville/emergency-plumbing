Plumbing Problems We Fix in Sioux Falls

Sioux Falls pipes take a beating: January cold snaps that freeze supply lines, hard water that scales heaters, and old clay sewer tile that roots find every spring. Here is what we get called for most and how we handle it.

01

Burst or frozen pipe at 2 a.m. in January

We shut off the main, thaw the line safely with controlled heat, and repair the split section with copper or PEX. Then we insulate the run and show you where to shut off water if it happens again.

02

Sewer backing up into the basement floor drain

We camera the line to find the blockage, hydro jet the grease or cut the root intrusion, and repair any cracked pipe we find. If the line is collapsed clay tile, we quote a replacement with footage to back it up.

03

No hot water before a holiday weekend

We test the element, thermostat, and dip tube, then repair or replace the unit the same day. Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Navien parts stay on the truck so you are not waiting on a warehouse.

04

Water bill doubled with no visible leak

That is usually a slab leak or a running toilet. We isolate the system, use a leak detection microphone to pinpoint the line, and repair the smallest section possible instead of tearing up the whole floor.
