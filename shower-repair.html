Tools and Methods We Use for Shower Repair

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

Pipe Wrench

Used to loosen and tighten supply line connections and valve bodies without stripping the fittings. Essential for older Sioux Falls homes with tight access behind the shower wall.

02

Drain Snake and Hydro Jetter

Clears hair, soap scum, and mineral blockages from the shower trap and branch line. The hydro jetter scours the pipe walls clean, restoring full flow.

03

Sewer Camera

Inspects the drain line after a backup to confirm the clog is gone and check for root intrusion or broken pipe. We show you the footage so you can see the problem.

Leak Detection Microphone

Listens for the sound of water escaping behind walls or under the pan. It pinpoints hidden leaks without tearing out tile unnecessarily.

05

Pressure Gauge

Confirms the supply line holds pressure after a repair. We test before and after to make sure the fix is solid.

06

PEX Crimping Tool

Used to make secure connections on PEX tubing when replacing supply lines. PEX is flexible and resists the corrosion that plagues older copper and galvanized pipe.

Why is my shower leaking in Sioux Falls South Dakota?

The most common cause is a failed valve cartridge or O-ring, which lets water seep behind the wall. Hard water in Sioux Falls accelerates that wear. A cracked drain gasket or loose shower arm connection can also leak. We pressure-test the line and inspect the pan to pinpoint it.

Can you repair a shower valve in Sioux Falls?

Yes. We repair and replace shower valves from Moen, Delta, Kohler, and American Standard. If the cartridge is the only failed part, we swap it in under an hour. If the valve body is corroded, we cut it out and solder in a new one, usually in two to three hours.

How long does shower repair take in Sioux Falls SD?

A cartridge or showerhead replacement takes one to two hours. A full valve body replacement runs two to four hours. If we're replacing a drain assembly or pan liner, plan on a half day. We give you a time estimate when we confirm the scope.
