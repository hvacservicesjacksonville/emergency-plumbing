Tools & methods

Tools and methods we use

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

RIDGID sewer camera

We push a camera through the line to see cracks, roots, bellies, and offset joints. You watch the footage on the monitor in real time so you see exactly what we see.

02

Hydro jetter

High-pressure water scours grease, scale, and roots from the pipe walls. It clears the line better than a drain snake and gives us a clean surface for the repair.

03

Pipe bursting equipment

For trenchless replacement, we pull a bursting head through the old pipe while pulling new HDPE pipe behind it. The old pipe breaks outward and the new line takes its place.

04

Leak detection microphone

We use it to listen for water escaping underground when the leak is not visible. It helps us pinpoint the break before we dig, which saves time and damage.

05

Milwaukee inspection tools

We carry Milwaukee drain augers, pipe cutters, and inspection lights on every truck. Reliable tools mean fewer trips back to the shop and faster repairs for you.

06

Pipe cutter and soldering torch

For spot repairs on copper or PVC, we cut the damaged section cleanly and solder or solvent-weld the new piece in place. Proper joints prevent future leaks.

How much does sewer line repair cost in Sioux Falls SD?

Most spot repairs run $400 to $1,200, and trenchless replacement runs $4,500 to $12,000. The price depends on depth, pipe length, material, and whether we can use trenchless methods. How do I know if my sewer line is broken in Sioux Falls South Dakota?

The most common signs are multiple slow drains, gurgling toilets, sewage smell in the basement, and wet or sunken spots in the yard. If only one fixture is slow, it is probably a local clog. If every drain in the house backs up at once, the problem is in the main line. A camera inspection confirms it in 30 minutes.

Can tree roots damage my sewer line in Sioux Falls?

Yes, and they are the number one cause of sewer line failures we see in older Sioux Falls neighborhoods. Roots find the smallest crack or joint and grow into the pipe looking for water. Over time they form a mat that catches everything and causes backups. We cut them out with a hydro jetter and repair the pipe so they cannot get back in.

Do you offer trenchless sewer repair in Sioux Falls SD?

We do. Trenchless sewer line repair Sioux Falls SD uses pipe bursting or cured-in-place lining to fix or replace the line through two small access pits. Your lawn, driveway, and landscaping stay intact. Most trenchless jobs finish in one day, and the new pipe carries a 5-year workmanship warranty.
