Common Drain Problems We Solve in Sioux Falls

From slow drains to full sewer backups, we've seen it all. Here are the most common issues we fix for homeowners and businesses in Sioux Falls.

We clear kitchen sink clogs with a drain snake or hydro jetter, then flush the line to remove all residue. We also inspect the P-trap and disposal for any contributing issues.

How much does drain cleaning cost in Sioux Falls SD?

Most residential drain cleaning jobs in Sioux Falls run $180 to $450 in 2026, depending on the clog's location and severity. Kitchen sinks are usually on the lower end. Main sewer line clogs with root intrusion cost more because they take longer and require heavier equipment. We give you a flat-rate price before we start, so there are no surprises.

How often should I get drain cleaning in Sioux Falls South Dakota?

For most homes, once every 12 to 18 months is enough to keep lines clear. If you have older cast-iron pipes or a history of root intrusion, we recommend an annual camera inspection and cleaning. Restaurants and commercial kitchens should schedule quarterly service because grease builds up fast.

What causes clogged drains in Sioux Falls homes?

The most common culprits are grease, hair, soap scum, and food particles. In older Sioux Falls neighborhoods, root intrusion from trees is a major cause. Winter freeze-thaw cycles can also shift pipes and create blockages. We see a lot of clogged drain repair Sioux Falls SD calls after heavy rain when stormwater overwhelms the main line.

Can you unclog a drain without harsh chemicals in Sioux Falls?

Yes. We never use chemical drain cleaners. Instead, we use mechanical tools like drain snakes, augers, and hydro jetters to physically remove the clog. This is safer for your pipes and more effective long-term. Chemical cleaners can corrode PVC and cast iron, leading to leaks and costly repairs down the road.
